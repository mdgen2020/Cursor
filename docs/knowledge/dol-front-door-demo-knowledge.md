Question:
What kinds of things can you help me with at the Department of Labor?

Answer:
The New York State Department of Labor virtual front door can help with **general information** about DOL services using approved knowledge articles. In this channel you can **schedule a new appointment** for eligible in-person services, **verify an existing appointment** when you have your **confirmation number** (identity verification is not required for verify-only), and get **personalized unemployment claim status** only **after** the assistant completes **identity verification** (for example your name and date of birth).

For **fraud prevention**, this automated channel **cannot cancel or reschedule** appointments. Cancellations and reschedules must be handled with a **Department of Labor representative**. If you have a confirmation number, the assistant may look up appointment details before connecting you to a representative.

Question:
How do I schedule a Department of Labor appointment?

Answer:
To schedule a new New York State Department of Labor appointment, the assistant collects the details needed to book your visit: preferred **date** and **time**, **office location or address**, your **contact name**, **phone number** and/or **email**, and the **reason** for the visit.

Appointments are typically offered on **weekdays, Monday through Friday**, during **office hours from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM** (local office time). After scheduling, you receive a **confirmation number**. Keep that number safe—you will need it to verify the appointment or when speaking with a representative about changes.

**In-person identity verification** for unemployment-related matters is a valid appointment reason. The assistant does not complete identity verification during the booking call itself; verification happens at the office or through separate verified steps when checking claim status by phone.

Question:
How do I check my unemployment claim status?

Answer:
**Personalized** unemployment claim status requires **identity verification** first. The assistant will ask for information such as your **full name** and **date of birth** before looking up your claim in approved systems.

Claim or incident reference numbers in demonstrations often begin with **INC** (for example INC0010123). After verification, the assistant explains the status returned by the system in **plain language**. The assistant **does not promise specific payment dates or dollar amounts** that are not returned by the backend systems. For disputes, missing payments, or complex issues, you may need to **speak with a Department of Labor representative**.

Question:
I have not received my unemployment check. What should I do?

Answer:
Treat a missing payment as a **claim status** concern. The assistant must **verify your identity** (such as name and date of birth) before discussing **your** claim. Payment timing varies by claim stage, banking, and program rules—the assistant cannot guarantee **when** a payment will arrive or **how much** you will receive unless that information is returned by the approved lookup tool.

If the status shows a delay, hold, or issue you disagree with, ask to **speak with a Department of Labor representative** for next steps. Do not share full Social Security numbers or bank details in unverified channels.

Question:
I need to come in to verify my identity for unemployment.

Answer:
You can **schedule an in-person appointment** with the reason **in-person identity verification**. Use weekday **office hours, Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM** (local office time). Bring **government-issued photo identification** and any documents your local New York DOL office requires; exact document lists can vary by location.

After booking, save your **confirmation number** to verify the appointment or when coordinating with a representative. Identity verification at the office is separate from phone-based identity verification used before claim status lookup.

What are Department of Labor office hours for appointments?

Answer:

On voice channels, keep answers brief: state the weekday window and offer to help schedule or verify an appointment if the resident is ready.

Question:
What is unemployment insurance?

Answer:
**Unemployment insurance** is a program that provides temporary income support to eligible workers who lose employment through no fault of their own, subject to state and federal rules. In **New York**, unemployment insurance is administered by the **New York State Department of Labor**.

This explanation is **general program information** only. **Your** eligibility, weekly benefit amount, payment dates, and claim decisions require **identity verification** and official systems—or a **representative**—not general knowledge alone.
