Question:
What are the hours for Massachusetts RMV service centers?

Answer:
Hours vary by RMV service center location. Many Massachusetts RMV service centers are open Monday through Friday during standard business hours, and some locations publish extended hours such as 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekdays.

Massachusetts RMV also offers dedicated appointment windows Monday through Friday between 9:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. at many service centers for customers age 65 or older and for customers with disabilities, for eligible license and ID transactions.

Notes:
- Always check the specific location page on Mass.gov before you travel. Hours can differ by office.
- Some locations offer Saturday hours for certain license, ID, or learner's permit transactions by appointment only.
- Road tests are by appointment only at locations that offer them, typically Monday through Friday during business hours.
- An appointment is required for many license and ID transactions even during posted walk-in hours.
