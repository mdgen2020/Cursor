Question:
How do I schedule an appointment at a Massachusetts RMV service center?

Answer:
You schedule most in-person RMV appointments online through the official myRMV appointment system on Mass.gov. Select **Make an Appointment** and choose the transaction you need, such as a driver's license, Mass ID, learner's permit, or an in-person vehicle registration or title transaction.

When appointment slots open, you may receive an email with a personalized scheduling link. That link is time-sensitive and can expire within about 24 hours, so check your email soon after you request an appointment.

Notes:
- License, learner's permit, and Mass ID transactions generally require an appointment.
- For vehicle registration and title transactions that must be done in person, you can schedule an appointment online. Walk-ins may be accepted at some locations, but customers with appointments are usually prioritized.
- If you cannot find an open time slot, try again later. Massachusetts RMV states that appointment times are released throughout the day.
- For general appointment availability at service centers, Massachusetts RMV lists an estimated wait of about 14 days to get an appointment slot, though times vary by location and transaction type.
