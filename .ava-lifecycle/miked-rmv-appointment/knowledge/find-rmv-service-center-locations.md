Question:
Where can I find a Massachusetts RMV service center near me?

Answer:
Use the official **RMV Service Center locations** page on Mass.gov to find Massachusetts Registry of Motor Vehicles offices. Each location page lists the street address, services offered, and any special notes such as Saturday hours, road tests, or business-to-business-only services.

When you ask about "where the RMV is located," this means the physical service center or branch that provides RMV transactions, not a virtual assistant or phone agent.

Notes:
- Not every service center offers every transaction. Check the location page for services before you go.
- Some locations are limited-service offices and cannot perform every registration or plate transaction.
- AAA members may complete select RMV transactions at participating AAA branches by appointment only.
- Schedule your appointment for the specific location you plan to visit when an appointment is required.
