Question:
What documents should I bring to register or title a vehicle in person at the Massachusetts RMV?

Answer:
For required in-person vehicle registration and title transactions, bring your completed application and supporting documents to the RMV service center where your appointment is scheduled. Commonly required items include:

- A completed registration and title application appropriate to your transaction.
- Proof of ownership, such as your prior title or bill of sale, depending on the transaction.
- Proof of Massachusetts auto insurance. Your insurance agent typically completes and stamps the application form required for registration.
- Your valid Massachusetts driver's license or other identification required for the transaction.
- Payment for applicable RMV fees.

Walk-ins may be accepted for some registration and title visits, but customers with scheduled appointments are usually prioritized.

Notes:
- Many registration renewals and replacements can be done online or by mail instead of in person. Check myRMV first.
- If a lender holds your title, the RMV may need to coordinate with the lienholder before issuing a Massachusetts title.
- Dealers, insurance agents, and registration runners often use separate business-to-business RMV services and may not use the same individual appointment process.
