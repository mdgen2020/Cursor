Question:
What RMV services can I complete online without visiting a service center?

Answer:
Many Massachusetts RMV transactions can be started or completed online through the official myRMV Online Service Center on Mass.gov. Online options often include renewing a driver's license or Mass ID, renewing or replacing a vehicle registration in eligible cases, ordering certain plates, paying some fees, updating your address, and accessing practice exams or road test scheduling where available.

Most myRMV individual online services require you to sign in with a MyMassGov account.

Notes:
- Online availability depends on your specific transaction and account status. myRMV will tell you if you must visit a service center instead.
- If myRMV shows that your transaction must be completed in person, schedule an appointment before you go.
- Use only official Mass.gov and myRMV websites. Massachusetts RMV warns customers about phishing and smishing scams impersonating the RMV.
