Question:
What documents should I bring to my Massachusetts RMV license, permit, or Mass ID appointment?

Answer:
Bring original documents, not copies or photos, unless Mass.gov specifically allows another format for your transaction. Requirements depend on the transaction, but common documents include:

- Proof of identity, such as a U.S. passport, certified birth certificate, or other federally acceptable identity document.
- Proof of Social Security number, such as your Social Security card, W-2, or pay stub showing your full Social Security number.
- Two proofs of Massachusetts residency, such as a utility bill, lease, mortgage statement, bank statement, or official mail showing your name and address.
- Your current out-of-state license if you are transferring from another state and the transaction requires it.
- Legal name-change documents if your current name differs from your identity document.

If you are applying for a REAL ID, you must verify documents in person at an RMV service center or at a participating AAA branch if you are a member.

Notes:
- Start or review your application online at myRMV when possible before your visit.
- Appointment requirements and document lists can change. Confirm the checklist for your exact transaction on Mass.gov before you go.
- If you are age 65 or older or have a disability, Massachusetts RMV offers dedicated appointment scheduling options described on Mass.gov.
