Question:
How do I cancel or reschedule my Massachusetts RMV appointment?

Answer:
Use the official myRMV appointment tools on Mass.gov to find or cancel an existing appointment when that option is available for your booking type. Sign in with the same account or email information you used when you scheduled the appointment.

If you need to change the date, time, or location, cancel the existing appointment if the system allows it, then schedule a new appointment for the transaction you need. Have your confirmation details ready when you contact official RMV support.

Notes:
- This virtual assistant can help you schedule or verify appointments, but changing or canceling an existing appointment may require transfer to a live agent or use of official RMV self-service tools.
- Do not share your confirmation code or personal documents with unofficial websites or unknown callers.
- For questions not answered online, use the official RMV customer contact form or published phone numbers on Mass.gov.
