Question:
How do I find or verify my existing RMV appointment?

Answer:
To find or manage an existing RMV appointment, use the official myRMV appointment tools on Mass.gov. From the RMV appointment page, choose the option to **find or cancel an existing appointment** and sign in if prompted.

If you booked through myRMV, check the email used when you scheduled the appointment. Massachusetts RMV may send messages that include your appointment details or a personalized link related to scheduling.

If you received a confirmation code when your appointment was booked, keep that code handy. You may be asked for it when verifying appointment details by phone or through a service that looks up your booking.

Notes:
- Bring your confirmation details and required documents to your appointment.
- If you cannot locate your appointment online, use the official RMV customer contact options on Mass.gov rather than sharing personal information with unofficial websites.
