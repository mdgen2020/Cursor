Product: Massachusetts Registry of Motor Vehicles (RMV)
Audience: End user / Customer
Region: Massachusetts
Channel: Voice / Digital
Last updated: 2026-09-29

Task:
Renew your vehicle registration with RMV self-service after identity verification

Purpose:
Use when your registration is nearing expiration and you are eligible to renew by phone or digital assistant with payment.

Prerequisites:
- You can verify your identity as required by the RMV.
- You know your plate number.
- Massachusetts insurance is active on the vehicle.
- Your mailing address on file is correct.

Procedure:
1. Verify your identity when prompted (name, date of birth, and any additional identifier requested).
2. Provide your license plate number when asked.
3. Confirm inspection status if asked; schedule inspection if yours is expired.
4. Confirm insurance is unchanged unless you updated it elsewhere.
5. Confirm the mailing address for registration documents and sticker.
6. Approve payment when quoted the renewal fee for your vehicle type.
7. Save the confirmation number given after successful payment.

Outcome:
You receive a confirmation number. Registration status updates in the RMV system. Registration certificate and sticker mail to your address on file within the stated mailing window.

Exceptions / Rules:
- If payment cannot be processed, retry later or ask for a representative.
- If the system shows a hold on your registration, resolve the hold before renewing.

Audience: End user / Customer
Region: Massachusetts
Channel: Voice / Digital
Last updated: 2026-09-29

Task:
Book an in-person RMV appointment for REAL ID or license renewal

Purpose:
Use when myRMV or the assistant directs you to complete license renewal or REAL ID document verification in person.

Prerequisites:
- You completed identity verification for self-service scheduling when required.
- You gathered REAL ID or renewal documents listed for your transaction.
- You received or will receive a confirmation code for the appointment when the system assigns one.

Procedure:
1. Say you want an in-person appointment for license renewal or REAL ID upgrade.
2. Confirm your preferred RMV service center or accept an offered location and date.
3. Confirm the appointment time and purpose (Standard renewal vs REAL ID).
4. Write down your appointment confirmation number.
5. Arrive about fifteen minutes early with original or acceptable printed documents.

Outcome:
You have a scheduled appointment with a confirmation number. At the visit, staff verify documents and complete eligible license work.

Exceptions / Rules:
- If no appointments are available, try another date or location, or check myRMV later.
- Phone photos of documents are not accepted at the counter when paper originals are required.
