Rule:
Identity verification before account-specific RMV help

Details:
When you ask about your license, registration, appointments, or other record-specific services, the RMV may verify your identity before discussing or changing your information. Verification commonly includes your full name, date of birth, and one additional identifier such as the last four digits of a phone number on file or other record match required for your channel.

After verification, you can receive self-service help for eligible transactions such as registration renewal or booking an appointment. If verification fails, you may retry with correct information or ask to speak with a representative.

Customer action:
Provide accurate identity information when asked. If you cannot verify, say you want an agent transfer for further help.

Rule:
Massachusetts auto insurance required for registration renewal

Details:
To renew a Massachusetts registration, the vehicle must be covered by valid Massachusetts automobile insurance for the registration period. When renewing by phone or online, you may be asked to confirm that insurance has not lapsed and that the same insurer remains on file unless you report a change through the proper channels.

Driving or renewing without required insurance can lead to suspension or other enforcement actions under Massachusetts law.

Customer action:
Confirm active insurance before renewing. If insurance lapsed, resolve insurance and any RMV suspension requirements before renewal.
