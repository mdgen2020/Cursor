# Acceptable proof of current rent for SNAP (Massachusetts DTA — demo knowledge)

**Audience:** Residents in an active SNAP recertification who must verify housing costs.  
**Demo abstraction:** Summarized from public DTA verification concepts; not a complete policy manual.

## What DTA typically accepts as proof of rent

You may provide **one** of the following that shows your name, address, amount paid, and period covered:

- Current **lease agreement** (signed) showing monthly rent
- **Rent receipt** from landlord for a recent month
- **Canceled check** or bank statement showing rent payment to landlord
- **Letter from landlord** on letterhead stating monthly rent amount and that you reside at the address (signed and dated)

The document should match the address on your SNAP case.

## What to do if you do not have a new lease

If rent increased but you do not yet have a new lease, you may still submit a **landlord letter** stating the new monthly amount. Complex rent changes may require a DTA worker to review — the assistant should not determine eligibility impact.

## What this knowledge is not

This card does **not** authorize benefit changes by itself. DTA staff systems record verified documents after intake.
