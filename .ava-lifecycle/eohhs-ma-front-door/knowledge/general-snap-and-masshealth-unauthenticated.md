# General information — SNAP and MassHealth (unauthenticated)

Use for **general** questions only. Do **not** use for case-specific status, deadlines, or eligibility outcomes.

## SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program)

- SNAP helps eligible households buy food through an EBT card.
- You apply through the **Department of Transitional Assistance (DTA)**. Massachusetts uses **BEACON** as the eligibility and case system behind DTA programs (not related to private health plans named Beacon).
- Typical application steps: submit an application, complete an interview, provide **verifications** (income, rent, identity) within allowed timeframes.
- **DTA Connect** is the Commonwealth’s online portal for applications and case tasks.

## MassHealth

- MassHealth is Massachusetts’ Medicaid program.
- A conversational assistant can help with **screening** and **starting an application** — only the **MassHealth agency** makes an **official eligibility determination** after a complete application and verification.
- Never tell a resident they are “approved” or “denied” based on screening alone.
